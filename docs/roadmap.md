# Research roadmap

This roadmap describes candidate research stages, not committed implementations or delivery dates.

## Stage 0 — Concept publication

- Publish the concept declaration.
- Establish the repository foundation.
- Record verified publication evidence.

## Stage 1 — Research definition

- Refine research questions, scope, and terminology.
- Define evidence standards.
- Develop an initial evaluation protocol.

## Stage 2 — Reproducible experiments

Candidate experiments may compare single-agent, router-based, and supervisor-based workflows using independently obtained public test environments. Public Linux/QEMU projects or similarly permissive environments may be considered after checking applicable permissions and reproducibility requirements.

These are candidate experiments, not committed implementations. Comparisons should state assumptions, evaluation criteria, and environment differences.

## Stage 3 — Research findings

- Publish methods and reproducible artifacts where permitted.
- Report negative and positive results.
- Record limitations and conditions affecting reproducibility.
