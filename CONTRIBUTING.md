# Contributing

The project is in its concept and research-definition phase. Research questions, relevant public references, reproducibility suggestions, and constructive technical discussion are welcome through issues.

Please distinguish evidence from assumptions and disclose known limitations. The BSP knowledge experiment accepts scoped code and content changes; see its README for validation commands and source requirements. General Research OS runtime development remains outside this experiment.

Do not submit confidential, proprietary, or unlawfully obtained materials. Only share materials you have permission to publish, and identify their public sources where applicable.
