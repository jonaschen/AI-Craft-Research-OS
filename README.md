# AI Craft Research OS

An independent research initiative by AI Craft: Agent Lab

AI Craft Research OS explores a human-directed approach to research with AI assistance. It studies how humans can coordinate AI capabilities to support research, implementation, verification, and review while retaining responsibility for research direction, independent verification, and reproducible evidence.

## Research principles

- **Model-independent:** Research methods and evidence should not depend exclusively on one AI model provider.
- **Evidence-driven:** Claims should be supported by inspectable artifacts, explicit evaluation methods, and reproducible experiments whenever possible.
- **Human-directed:** Humans own the research questions, boundaries, judgment, and final decisions.

## Project status

**Status: Research with an executable BSP knowledge experiment**

No operational Research OS implementation is claimed. The repository now includes a scoped [BSP knowledge MVP](experiments/bsp-kb/README.md): Traditional Chinese lessons, offline retrieval, deterministic human/agent bundles, stateless UART signature analysis, source provenance, tests and a development benchmark. This experiment does not demonstrate a complete Research OS or general agent capability.
